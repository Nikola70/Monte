Rooms

Wake up to the golden glow of the Caucasus peaks from your own private balcony at Hotel Monte. Our thoughtfully designed rooms come in a range of categories—from intimate doubles for couples seeking a romantic escape, to expansive family rooms where everyone can unwind together in comfort. Soft bedding, warm interiors, and modern amenities create a haven you will look forward to returning to after a day on the slopes or exploring Gudauri. Here, every room is not just a place to stay—it is part of your mountain experience.

In every room

  • Private balcony with mountain view
  • Private bathroom with shower, bidet, bathrobes, slippers, towels and hairdryer
  • Flat-screen TV with satellite channels
  • Free Wi-Fi
  • Tea and coffee maker and electric kettle
  • Refrigerator
  • In-room safe
  • Desk and wardrobe
  • Heating
  • Mosquito net
  • Wake-up service
  • Non-smoking rooms
  • Access to the steam room, Finnish sauna, indoor pool and game room
  • Shuttle to the Shino ski lift during the winter season
