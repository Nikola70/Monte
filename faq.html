Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what guests ask us most. If you cannot find yours, please contact us and we will be happy to help.

Planning and booking
How do I make a reservation?

Use the reservation form on the Contact page, or reach us by phone, WhatsApp or email. We will get back to you soon, and we look forward to welcoming you in the mountains!

How early should I book, and how can I see the price?

Gudauri fills up fast over New Year, on weekends and during holidays in the ski season, so book as early as you can. Prices depend on the season and the room. Choose a room and your dates in the reservation form on the Contact page and it shows an estimated total for your stay. You can also write to us for availability and prices.

Which currencies can I pay in?

Reservations can be made and paid in Georgian Lari (GEL). International transfers can also be made in US Dollars (USD) or Euros (EUR).

Can I pay by card, or should I bring cash?

At our hotel we accept both cash and cards. Many other hotels and larger shops also accept cards, but small cafés, kiosks and taxis often prefer cash in Georgian Lari (GEL). There are several ATMs in Gudauri, but it is still a good idea to bring some cash.

Can we have rooms next to each other for a family or group?

It depends on availability and on the types of room you book. It is best to ask us, and we will tell you whether it is possible.

Do you offer half board?

If you reserve at least 10 rooms, we can offer you half board. Please ask us for more details.

Getting here
How do I get from Tbilisi to Gudauri?

Gudauri is about 120 km from Tbilisi, usually a drive of 2 to 2.5 hours depending on traffic and weather. You can come by private transfer or taxi, by rental car, or by shared minibus (marshrutka) from Tbilisi’s Didube bus station. From Tbilisi International Airport, a private transfer or taxi straight to Gudauri is the most convenient option. If you drive, free parking is available at our hotel. Please contact us before you arrive and we will gladly advise you.

Can you arrange a transfer from the airport or other places?

Yes, we can arrange a transfer between the hotel and Tbilisi International Airport, Kutaisi International Airport, or any other place in Tbilisi or elsewhere in Georgia. For exact prices, please ask us by phone or email and we will provide more details.

Do I need snow chains or a 4x4 in winter?

If you drive yourself in winter, expect snow and ice. During the winter months, winter tyres are required by law, so if you rent a car, make sure it is equipped with winter tyres. A 4x4 and snow chains are preferable but not mandatory. After heavy snowfall, however, the authorities may introduce temporary requirements and restrict access for two-wheel-drive cars unless they have chains. Please check road conditions before you set off, because the road can close for hours after heavy snow. If you prefer not to drive, a private transfer with a local driver is the easier choice.

Is parking available?

Yes, free parking is available at the hotel for our guests.

Your stay
What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is until 12:00.

Can I check in early or check out late?

It depends on availability, so it cannot be guaranteed. Early check-in and late check-out are granted only at the hotel’s discretion, so please request them in advance and wait for our confirmation. Charges may apply if approved; see the Terms and Policy.

Can I store my luggage before check-in or after check-out?

Yes, this is possible, free of charge.

Do children stay free?

Children 5 years of age and younger stay free of charge, provided no extra bed is used.

Do you provide a baby crib?

Yes, we provide a baby crib free of charge for children 5 years of age and younger, upon request.

Can I add an extra bed to my room?

Yes, for an additional charge. One extra bed is available in the Large Double, Superior Double and Superior Family rooms, and up to two in the Deluxe Double and the Suite. See all rooms.

What is included in the room price?

Room rates include VAT and breakfast, the shuttle to the Shino ski lift (during the ski season only), and access to the saunas, swimming pool and game room. Every room has a balcony with a mountain view. Compare all rooms.

Do the rooms have a balcony and Wi-Fi?

Yes. Every room has a private balcony with a mountain view and free Wi-Fi.

Should I get a local SIM card for mobile internet?

Every room has free Wi-Fi. For mobile data on the road and in the mountains, it is worth buying a local SIM card or eSIM (for example from Magti or Silknet) when you arrive in Tbilisi.

Is the hotel wheelchair friendly?

Yes, the hotel has a lift and it is wheelchair friendly. We also have a room adapted for disabled guests. Please contact us for details.

Do the rooms have air conditioning?

The rooms have heating but no air conditioning.

Are pets allowed?

Unfortunately, pets are not allowed at the hotel.

Food and facilities
Is breakfast included?

Yes. Room rates include breakfast.

What food do you serve?

Breakfast includes vegetarian dishes, pastries, fruit, hot meals and traditional Georgian specialties. Our restaurant serves Georgian and European cuisine.

Is there a restaurant and bar?

Yes. Our restaurant serves Georgian and European cuisine, and our lounge bar has a cozy fireplace and serves Georgian wines, cocktails and light snacks. Both have terraces with views of the Caucasus Mountains, so you can enjoy your meal or drink indoors or outside. Read more on our Dining page.

Is there a sauna or a pool?

Yes. Guests can use the steam and Finnish saunas and the small indoor pool free of charge. No booking is required. See all facilities.

Is laundry service available?

Yes. Please ask at reception for prices.

Skiing and activities
When does the winter season start and end?

It depends on the snow level. Generally the ski season opens some time in mid-December and finishes in mid-April. For the 2026/27 season, the opening has been announced for December 20th.

How far is the hotel from the ski lifts, and is there a shuttle?

The nearest ski lift, Shino, is about 700 metres away. All the lifts and slopes in Gudauri are interconnected, so from Shino you can reach any lift you want to use. A free shuttle for our guests to the Shino ski lift also runs during the ski season, so you can reach the slopes without driving. See the route from the hotel to Shino on Google Maps.

Is the hotel ski-in/ski-out?

Our hotel is not a ski-in/ski-out hotel. However, as an extra option, you can ski down the slopes, or off-piste, to a point close to the hotel, and from there it is only a few minutes’ walk to the hotel. We also provide a free shuttle service for our guests during the ski season.

Can I rent skis or a snowboard?

Yes, you can rent skis and snowboards directly at the hotel. We also provide helmets and goggles.

Is there a place to store my ski equipment?

Yes, guests can store their ski equipment in our ski depot.

Can I book ski lessons?

Private instructors and ski-school teams give lessons for beginners and improvers right on the Gudauri slopes. Lessons are paid, so please ask at reception for details.

What can I do in Gudauri besides skiing?

Skiing and snowboarding, paragliding, hiking, rafting and day trips to sights such as Gergeti Trinity Church, among much else. See our Things to Do guide.
