Facilities

At Hotel Monte, we make your stay as comfortable, convenient, and enjoyable as possible. In winter, we offer a complimentary shuttle service to the nearby Shino ski lift, making it easy to hit the slopes. Guests can also rent high-quality ski and snowboard equipment directly from the hotel, so you are always ready for a day of adventure. For relaxation after an active day, unwind in our steam room or warm up in our Finnish sauna.


Year-round, you can enjoy our well-equipped game room, indoor pool, and mountain bike rentals for exploring the beautiful surroundings in summer. Our conference room provides the ideal space for meetings and corporate events, while our friendly staff is always ready to assist with any special requests. Whether you are here for adventure, business, or pure relaxation, Hotel Monte has everything you need for an unforgettable stay.

Spa

Relax and rejuvenate in our spa, featuring a steam room, a Finnish sauna and a refreshing pool to melt away stress after a day of adventure.

Ski Rental

Rent high-quality skis and snowboards directly at the hotel, so you’re always ready to hit the slopes without hassle.

Game Room

Have fun year-round in our game room, equipped with table tennis, billiards, table football, chess and other games for guests of all ages.

Shuttle to Ski Lift Shino

Enjoy our free winter shuttle service to the nearby Shino ski lift, making your ski days even more convenient.
