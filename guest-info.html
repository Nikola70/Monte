Your Room

Every room has a balcony with a mountain view.

Breakfast

Complimentary breakfast is served daily, with vegetarian dishes, pastries, fruits, hot meals and traditional Georgian specialties.

Hours: 09:00 – 11:00

Restaurant

Georgian and European cuisine made from fresh, local ingredients. Dine indoors or on our spacious terraces with views of the Caucasus Mountains.

Hours: 17:00 – 22:00 (restaurant and lounge bar)

Lounge Bar

A stylish bar with a cozy fireplace and a terrace, serving Georgian wines, cocktails and light snacks.

Hours: 17:00 – 22:00

Spa and Pool

Steam and Finnish saunas, a small indoor pool and professional massage services.

Saunas and pool: 17:00 – 22:00. No booking is required during these hours.

Game Room

Activities and entertainment for guests of all ages.

Hours: 09:00 – 23:00
