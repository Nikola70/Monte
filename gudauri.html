Things to Do

From Ananuri Fortress on the Aragvi River to the Russian border at Upper Lars, the Georgian Military Road is lined with castles, monasteries, waterfalls and mountain trails, and Gudauri sits right in the middle of it. Here is what you can do and see from Hotel Monte.

Distances are by road from the hotel. Use the filters to plan by season, hiking effort and cost, and open any place in Google Maps.

Prices, opening hours and seasons are approximate and change often, so please ask at reception before you set off. In winter the Military Road can close for hours after heavy snow: check road conditions. Carry your passport near the border (Truso, Dariali). Mountain trails need proper footwear and a weather check; take a guide for glaciers, ski touring and ice.

  • Ananuri Fortress

    Ananuri to Pasanauri

    • ❄️☀️ Year-round
    • 🚗 No hiking needed
    • Free

    57 km · about 58 min by car from the hotel

    A 16th–17th-century fortress and church complex on the shore of the Zhinvali Reservoir, once the seat of the Dukes of Aragvi. Climb the tower for views over the water.

    Good to know: Entry is free; opening hours vary by source. Cover shoulders and knees inside the church. Icy in winter.

  • Arsha Waterfalls

    Kobi to Kazbegi and Dariali

    • ❄️☀️ Year-round
    • 🥾 Moderate hike · 15–45 min
    • Free

    26 km · about 23 min by car to the starting point

    Two cascades on the Arshistskhali River near the village of Arsha, a short drive from Stepantsminda. The first waterfall appears suddenly as you approach; the second, called “Woman’s Hair”, flows down a large boulder. The ruins of Arsha Fortress stand on the ridge above.

    Good to know: The first waterfall is about 15 minutes on foot from the parking area, an easy walk. The second waterfall is another 30 minutes from the first, a moderate hike. Wear sturdy shoes. Best in summer and autumn; possible in other months if there is little snow. The distance shown is to Arsha village, where the trail starts.

    Directions from Monte

  • Khada Valley

    Ananuri to Pasanauri

    • ☀️ Summer
    • 🥾 Moderate hike · 2–3 h
    • Free

    19 km · about 19 min by car to the starting point

    A scenic side valley above Kvesheti, known as the valley of towers. Things to see include old stone defensive towers, the 10th–11th-century Korogho church, an iron-rich mineral spring and a small waterfall, with views over the gorge. Reached on foot or by a dirt track from Kvesheti.

    Good to know: Best in summer; nearly impassable in winter. You need an off-road (4x4) car for the dirt track. The distance shown is to Kvesheti, where the track starts.

  • Rock Climbing in Gveleti

    Kobi to Kazbegi and Dariali

    • ☀️ Summer
    • 🚗 No hiking needed
    • Paid

    40 km · about 35 min by car from the hotel

    A sport-climbing crag near the Gveleti waterfalls with bolted routes about 22 to 28 m long, graded around 6a to 6a+. The rock is in the shade from about 13:00, which makes it a good choice for hot summer days. The walk from the parking spot is only about 50 m.

    Good to know: Bring a 60 m rope, about 15 quickdraws and a helmet. Ask at reception to arrange a guide. Gveleti is also known for ice climbing in winter (see the Ice Climbing card).

  • Truso Valley

    Kobi to Kazbegi and Dariali

    • ☀️ Summer
    • 🥾 Moderate hike · 1.5–7 h
    • Free entry, paid extras

    15 km · about 15 min by car to the starting point

    A high, almost flat valley of white travertine terraces, mineral springs, stone towers and abandoned villages, reached on a dirt road from Kobi. On the way you can see the Abano mineral lake, the village of Ketrisi with its nunnery, old stone towers and, at the far end, the ruined Zakagori fortress.

    Cost: Entry is free; a taxi or guided 4x4 is paid (roughly 40 GEL one way by taxi, approx.).

    Good to know: Open roughly late May–October. The travertines are about 1.5 hours on foot from the road end; the whole valley out and back takes 5–7 hours. Carry your passport: border guards check it at the valley entrance. The distance shown is to Kobi, where the Truso road turns off.

    Directions from Monte
