Gudauri

Nestled high in the majestic Caucasus Mountains, Gudauri is one of Georgia’s premier mountain resorts, offering breathtaking natural beauty and an abundance of outdoor activities for every season. Whether you’re seeking adrenaline-pumping adventure or peaceful relaxation, Gudauri has something to captivate every traveler.


During winter, Gudauri transforms into a skier’s and snowboarder’s paradise. With vast, well-groomed slopes suitable for all skill levels, modern ski lifts, and reliable snowfall, it’s a favorite destination for both beginners and seasoned winter sports enthusiasts. Off the slopes, cozy mountain lodges and lively après-ski spots create a vibrant atmosphere perfect for unwinding.


When the snow melts, Gudauri reveals lush alpine meadows and rugged mountain trails ideal for hiking, mountain biking, and paragliding. The warm summer months invite visitors to explore nature’s wonders, discover hidden waterfalls, and enjoy panoramic views that stretch as far as the eye can see.


Beyond its natural charms, Gudauri is a gateway to authentic Georgian culture. Visitors can savor traditional cuisine, meet friendly locals, and experience the renowned Georgian hospitality that makes every stay memorable. Nearby villages and towns offer cultural sights, crafts, and festivals that enrich the mountain experience.


Whether you’re planning a family holiday, a romantic getaway, or an adventurous trip with friends, Gudauri’s diverse offerings ensure an unforgettable escape. From thrilling winter sports to serene summer landscapes and rich cultural encounters, Gudauri stands as a destination that truly has it all.
